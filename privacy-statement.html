01

Introduction

Operix is an enterprise resource planning (ERP) platform built for internal business operations. It covers inventory management, purchase order processing, order fulfillment, supplier management, financial ledger functions, accounts payable and receivable, and multi-step approval workflows.

This Privacy Statement explains how Operix collects, uses, stores, protects, and manages information when administrators, approvers, and authorized personnel access and operate the system.

By accessing or using Operix, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the data practices described in this Privacy Statement. If you do not agree, please contact your system administrator.

This statement applies to all Operix modules including Inventory, Orders, Purchases, Receiving, Suppliers, Shipping, General Ledger, Payables, Receivables, Reports, and the Approval Worklist.

02

Information We Collect

Operix collects information necessary to operate and maintain the system's business processes. The categories of information collected are:

Account & Identity Information

  • Full name, email address, and role designation
  • Assigned approval authorities and step positions within approval groups
  • Profile photographs (where uploaded)
  • Digital signature images uploaded by or on behalf of administrators for use in official PO documents

Operational & Transaction Data

  • Purchase orders, revision history, approval chains, and PO status records
  • Supplier records including names, addresses, contact details, banking references (encrypted), and TIN/Tax IDs (encrypted)
  • Inventory items, stock levels, SKUs, cost prices, and category assignments
  • Customer orders, quotations, shipping records, and receiving logs
  • Payment terms, currency settings, and delivery notes

Financial Records

  • General ledger journal entries and account mappings
  • Accounts payable and receivable balances
  • Tax computations, VAT amounts, subtotals, discounts, and grand totals
  • Revenue figures and order amounts used in dashboard analytics

Audit & Activity Logs

  • Timestamps of all system actions (approvals, denials, revisions, status changes)
  • User identifiers associated with each recorded action
  • Approval history entries including step index, approver name, role, and decision date
  • IP addresses and session identifiers for security audit purposes
03

How We Use Your Information

Information collected within Operix is used exclusively for the following operational and compliance purposes:

  • Authenticate users and enforce access based on assigned roles and permission tiers
  • Process, track, and advance purchase orders through configured multi-step approval workflows
  • Manage supplier relationships, inventory stock levels, pricing, and restocking thresholds
  • Fulfill customer orders, track shipments, and log receiving transactions
  • Generate financial reports, journal ledger entries, and management dashboards
  • Maintain complete, tamper-evident audit trails required for financial compliance and accountability
  • Render official PDF purchase order documents with assigned approver signatures
  • Deliver in-system notifications for approval requests, status changes, and workflow events
  • Monitor system access and enforce security policies
Operix does not use collected data for advertising, user profiling, behavioral analytics, or any purpose outside the direct operation of your organization's business processes.
04

Role-Based Access Controls

Access to data within Operix is strictly governed by a role-based access control (RBAC) model. Each user is granted access only to the information necessary for their designated function.

Role Access Scope Key Capabilities
Master Administrator Full system access User management, approval group configuration, signature assignment, system-wide settings, all modules
Administrator Assigned modules only Operational module access as assigned; can process POs, orders, inventory; limited settings access
Approver Approval worklist & assigned POs View, approve, or deny purchase orders routed to their approval step
Standard User / Purchaser Assigned module scope Create and submit requests within permitted modules; no access to system settings or other users' data

All role assignments and permission changes are logged and auditable. No user may access, modify, or export data beyond the boundaries of their defined role.

05

Data Storage & Security

All Operix data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure through Firebase and Firestore services, which operate under Google's enterprise-grade security and compliance frameworks. The following measures are in place:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS protocols at all times
  • Data at rest is encrypted by the Google Cloud infrastructure using AES-256
  • Sensitive supplier fields — including banking references and Tax ID numbers — are additionally encrypted at the application layer using AES encryption before being written to the database
  • Digital signature images are stored in Firebase Storage with access restricted by Firebase Authentication tokens; no unauthenticated access is possible
  • Session management is handled through Firebase Authentication with automatic token expiry on logout and inactivity timeout
  • Passwords are managed exclusively through Firebase Authentication; no plaintext passwords are stored anywhere in the system
  • All database read/write operations are protected by Firestore Security Rules scoped to authenticated users
Operix's security practices are reviewed periodically. If a security incident affecting your data is identified, your organization's Master Administrator will be notified promptly.
06

Data Sharing & Disclosure

Operix does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer personal or operational data to external parties. Data is shared only in the following strictly limited circumstances:

  • Internal Operations: Data is shared among authorized users within your organization solely as required to complete business processes such as approval routing, document generation, and fulfillment workflows.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Google Firebase and Google Cloud Platform serve as the underlying infrastructure. These services operate under Google's enterprise Data Processing Addendum and are subject to their own privacy and security commitments.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose data if required to do so by applicable law, lawful regulation, court order, or request from a competent government authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of business assets, data may be transferred to the successor entity. The successor will be bound by equivalent or stronger privacy protections.

Suppliers and external parties to whom purchase orders are issued receive only the information contained in the generated PO document. They do not receive any access to the Operix system or its underlying data.

07

Signature & Document Data

Operix generates official PDF purchase order documents that may include digital signature images belonging to administrators who serve as designated approvers. These signatures are handled as follows:

  • Signature images are uploaded voluntarily by or on behalf of the named administrator through the Master Dashboard
  • Signatures are used solely for rendering official purchase order output documents
  • Signature images are stored securely in Firebase Storage with access tokens restricted to authenticated system users only
  • Signatures are never shared with suppliers, external parties, or third-party services in raw form — they appear only as embedded images in generated PDF documents
  • When a PO is generated, the system fetches the approver's signature using their approverAdminId stored in the approval group configuration to ensure the correct signature is always matched to the correct approver step
Any administrator may request the removal or replacement of their signature image at any time by contacting the system's Master Administrator. Removal does not affect historical PO documents that have already been generated.
08

Data Retention

Operix retains data according to the following schedules, designed to meet standard accounting, audit, and operational requirements:

Data Category Description Retention Period
Purchase Orders & Approval History All PO records, revisions, and complete approval chains 7 years minimum
Financial & Ledger Records Journal entries, AP/AR balances, tax computations 7 years minimum
Inventory Transaction Logs Receiving entries, stock adjustments, order fulfillment 5 years minimum
User Account Data Profile, role assignments, signature references Duration of active authorization + 2 years post-deactivation
System Access Logs Session records, IP addresses, authentication events 90 days minimum
Notification Records Approval requests, status change alerts 1 year

Requests for early deletion of specific records must be submitted to the Master Administrator and are subject to applicable legal and regulatory retention requirements. Some records may not be eligible for early deletion if they form part of an ongoing audit trail.

10

Cookies & Session Management

Operix is a web-based single-page application. It does not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or behavioral analytics tools of any kind.

  • Authentication state is managed through Firebase Authentication tokens stored in browser session storage
  • Session tokens expire automatically on logout or after the inactivity timeout configured by your administrator
  • No persistent cross-session tracking cookies are set
  • Browser local storage may be used for ephemeral UI preferences (such as sidebar state) that contain no personal data
Operix products are ad-free. No advertiser pays to have any product, service, or content promoted within the system.
